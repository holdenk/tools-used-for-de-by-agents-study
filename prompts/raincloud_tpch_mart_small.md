Build a sales data mart from the TPC-H tables.

- Input: the eight TPC-H tables at scale factor 1, generated by tpchgen-rs and
  served by Raincloud (https://github.com/spiraldb/raincloud) as the datasets
  `tpcgen-rs-tpch-sf1-<table>` (lineitem, orders, customer, part, partsupp,
  supplier, nation, region), as Parquet or Vortex files. In production the
  files come from our team's Raincloud mirror (`RAINCLOUD_MIRROR`, an `s3://`
  prefix); locally you can build them with Raincloud.
- Scale: SF1 is 8.7 million rows across the eight tables, 6 million of them in
  lineitem (about 240 MB as Parquet). The mart rebuilds nightly on one small
  VM (4 vCPUs, 16 GB RAM).
- Output, with the fact table partitioned by order month: a `fact_sales` table
  at line-item grain (net revenue = extendedprice * (1 - discount), tax, ship
  delay in days, a late flag when receipt is after commit) with conformed
  `dim_customer` (with nation and region), `dim_supplier`, `dim_part` and
  `dim_date` dimensions; plus monthly revenue by region, nation and market
  segment, quarterly on-time delivery rate per supplier, and the top 10 parts
  by revenue per brand per year.
- Money stays exact decimal (no floats). The fact table reconciles to lineitem
  (row count and revenue totals), TPC-H Q1 and Q5 (with the spec's validation
  parameters) return the same results against the mart as against the base
  tables, and rerunning a load is idempotent.
- Include tests on a small fixture with the same schema, a README with the run
  / schedule command, RUN the tests, and run the whole pipeline once on the
  real SF1 data (`tpcgen-rs-tpch-sf1-<table>`) before you stop. If you can't
  build the data, say so in the README.
